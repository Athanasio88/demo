## Prompt utilizado

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: polished horizontal brand artwork for the top of a dark industrial website, final standalone PNG with real transparent alpha background. The attached image is the brand identity reference, not a layout to reproduce. Create an elegant horizontal signature inspired closely by this exact JC Alvos Metálicos logo. On the LEFT, preserve the recognizable sculptural silver JC monogram proportions and cutouts from the reference, with its three small solid colored circles arranged above it: yellow at the top, red below-left, orange below-right. On the RIGHT, two aligned lines of raised brushed stainless-steel lettering, exact Portuguese text: 'ALVOS' then 'METÁLICOS', in a clean strong industrial sans serif. The monogram and lettering form one cohesive, compact, wide composition, approximately 3:1 aspect ratio. Realistic matte steel, subtle machining grain and shallow edge bevels, restrained warm reflected light, crisp readable edges suitable on charcoal background. It should feel like quality fabricated metal signage, traditional and grounded, NOT futuristic. Do NOT include the large circular backing plate, no enclosing circle, no floating medallion, no rectangular backing plaque, no background scene, no mockup webpage, no glow, no neon, no weapons or additional objects, no other words, no watermark. Ensure all of the artwork fits well inside the image with modest transparent margins. Preserve actual transparency, not a checkerboard drawn into the image.
